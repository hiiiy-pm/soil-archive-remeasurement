# Quality-control amendment

Recorded after the first as-released replay and before either quality-controlled replay. The as-released analysis exposed an impossible carbon mass and further discordant tins. The journal's original S1 Dataset (10.1371/journal.pone.0311364.s007) and HF449 give the same tin masses for PS54 and PS07. In PS54 tin 2, 34,020 micrograms of carbon in 31.382 mg of dry soil exceeds the dry mass. No decimal point, mass or replicate is imputed.

Report the physically admissible replay as the main external estimate. Retain the original replay in the supplement as an audit. Keep the calibration plots, auxiliary calibration coefficients, single-tin SDs, two-sided multipliers, comparison targets, seeds and allocation rules as specified in the original protocol.

1. For the physical screen, a valid specimen must have both tin concentrations between 0 and 1,000 g C/kg dry soil, inclusive. Exclude its entire plot–horizon pair when either month fails. As in the original design, a reported experimental cell must have at least two matched plots. Remove cells below that minimum from both the acquisition frame and scoring. Recompute the mean limit with the original formula if the number of retained plots changes. List every record and reason. No reference value from an excluded pair enters model fitting or acquisition.
2. For a separate duplicate-precision sensitivity, also remove any specimen with an absolute tin difference greater than sqrt(2) × s × t(1 − 0.05/(2 × 42), m), using the original horizon calibration and the original 42 test specimens. This two-sided Bonferroni screening limit flags extreme within-specimen disagreement while accounting for the number of test specimens screened. Apply the same matched-pair and two-plot rules. The probability interpretation assumes independent, approximately normal tin errors within each horizon.
3. All strategies within a replay see the same record eligibility flags and the same prior LOI values. These are retrospective quality-controlled frames. They assume laboratory quality flags are available when defining the archive. Recompute all selections and predictions from scratch separately for each frame. Report the original and both quality-controlled results, including all comparators and their ordering. The additional screens were chosen after inspecting the original data. They are not part of the original locked specification.

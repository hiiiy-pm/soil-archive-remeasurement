# Independent multiseason replay

This local design was written before comparing any sampling policies on the new archive. It is a retrospective reanalysis. It has no public preregistration timestamp.

Use one independent archive with actual technical replicates of the same soil material, at least three field collection campaigns spanning at least three meteorological seasons, two measured depth or horizon strata, and a reference-independent auxiliary available under the replay's stated cost convention. Prefer a solid-soil chemical measurement. Select the site, time window, assay, auxiliary and QC restrictions using source metadata, laboratory documentation and completeness alone. Record those decisions, the calibration data, split and numerical analytical limits before crossing the scoring boundary. Archive field provenance and document any failed candidates. Do not tune a rule after calculating test losses.

Choose the plots, dates or specimens used to estimate repeatability separately from held-out specimens. For independent, approximately normal and centred determination errors, estimate single-assay variance from within-specimen variation. Let s be the single-assay SD and v the residual degrees of freedom. For a contrast with linear specimen weights c_i and r_i exchangeable determinations averaged for specimen i, use the two-sided analytical limit t(0.975,v) s sqrt(sum_i c_i^2/r_i). Use horizon-specific precision where the archive supports it; record any pooling and its rationale before scoring. The score includes a contrast only if its absolute measured change exceeds its fixed limit. Where the reference is the first determination rather than an average, use r_i=1. This is an analytical repeatability limit conditional on observed specimens.

Hold the proportional stratum sequence identical for all policies after a shared simple-random pilot. At each step choose the stratum making cumulative counts closest to eligible frame proportions, with a shared seed for tied strata. Within the allowed stratum evaluate the existing joint maximin, magnitude-only, direct sign, random-without-replacement and alternating rules. Average the two alternating orders within a pilot. Use the existing common ordinary-kriging reconstruction and nugget ratio one. Use 200 shared starts (seeds 736400 to 736599), ten budgets at rounded 10% increments, and the mean over the nine partial budgets. Verify exact census recovery.

The primary sign score averages eligible seasonal changes within each stratum and gives each represented stratum equal weight. The prespecified primary comparison is joint minus magnitude on this score. The second endpoint is the original domain- and family-weighted scaled MSE on all supported comparisons, with joint versus direct sign as its prespecified comparison. Report all five strategies on both metrics, per-stratum and per-budget results, counts of excluded or unsupported contrasts, and an equal-contrast sensitivity. Report paired Monte Carlo intervals across starts, conditional on the fixed archive. To cover the two main comparisons simultaneously, use two-sided 97.5% individual paired t intervals (Bonferroni familywise 95%). Preserve null and adverse findings.
